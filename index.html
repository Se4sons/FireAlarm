How it works
Three steps, fully paperless
No app installs needed. Tenants just scan and sign.
1
Register your property
Add the address and every alarm in the unit. Each unit gets one QR code that covers all alarms.
2
Print and attach the QR
Print from the admin panel and stick it inside the unit door or near the alarms. Any phone camera can scan it.
3
Scan, test, and sign
Tenant opens the form, follows testing steps, signs off. It hits your dashboard in real time.
Features
Everything in one place
Built for landlords and property managers.

Admin login required
